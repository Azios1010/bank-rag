## 1. Status and scope

This is a complete synthetic internal product policy of Ngân hàng Thương mại Cổ phần Hồng Hà (HHB), a fictional internal organization. `synthetic=true`: its values are not Vietnamese law, are not universal banking requirements, and must be read only as HHB internal policy.

Rule ID: PROD-STATUS. This policy governs the unsecured SME working-capital product. It does not provide a legal SME classification test.

## 2. Target customer and ordinary use

Rule ID: PROD-TARGET. The target customer is a registered Vietnamese SME business with transparent ownership, lawful operating activity, and a demonstrated working-capital need. The relationship manager must identify the legal entity, beneficial ownership, management, business history, and operating cycle; underwriting verification is governed by [[UW-IDENTITY-AND-INDUSTRY]].

Rule ID: PROD-PURPOSE. Permitted purposes are inventory, trade receivables, payroll, rent, utilities, and ordinary operating expenses tied to the borrower's business cycle. A proposed use must be evidenced by a purpose budget and supporting invoices or contracts, then tested against cash flow under [[UW-CASH-FLOW]].

Rule ID: PROD-EXCLUSIONS. Capital expenditure, real-estate or securities speculation, crypto-asset trading, owner distributions, repayment of overdue debt, and any purpose rejected by legal/compliance review are prohibited. Gambling, unlicensed financial intermediation, weapons trafficking, adult-content operations, crypto-asset speculation, and speculative real-estate trading are excluded industries. These are hard stops and cannot be waived under [[APR-HARD-STOPS]].

## 3. Standard eligibility and product structure

Rule ID: PROD-HISTORY. Standard eligibility requires at least 24 completed months of operating history before application. A documented soft exception may accept 18–23 completed months; history below 18 months is a hard stop under [[APR-HARD-STOPS]].

Rule ID: PROD-REVENUE. Standard eligibility requires annual revenue of at least VND 10 billion, measured from the latest completed fiscal year and corroborated by trailing-12-month evidence. A documented soft exception may accept VND 8 billion to less than VND 10 billion; revenue below VND 8 billion is a hard stop.

Rule ID: PROD-EXPOSURE. Maximum total unsecured exposure is VND 5 billion per borrower. Total exposure includes the proposed facility plus existing HHB funded and committed obligations and applicable guarantees or contingent obligations, as defined by [[APR-EXPOSURE]]. Amounts above VND 5 billion are outside this product.

Rule ID: PROD-TENOR. Maximum tenor is 12 months. A renewal is a new review, not an automatic extension, and is subject to [[APR-REAPPROVAL]].

Rule ID: PROD-REPAYMENT. Permitted structures are: (a) an amortizing term facility with monthly interest and at least quarterly principal; or (b) a revolving line with monthly interest and a clean-down to zero for 30 consecutive days within each 12-month period. Bullet repayment is prohibited.

Rule ID: PROD-COLLATERAL. No collateral is required or taken for this product. Personal guarantees do not turn it into secured exposure and do not relax financial thresholds; repayment capacity is assessed without collateral support under [[UW-REPAYMENT-CAPACITY]].

## 4. Evidence, CIC, and renewal

Rule ID: PROD-DOCUMENTS. Required documents are registration and authority evidence; ownership and management information; two completed fiscal-year financial packages where available; latest interim or trailing-12-month data; 12-month bank statements; tax/revenue corroboration; a debt schedule; and a purpose budget with supporting invoices or contracts. Missing or contradictory material evidence is a hold/refer outcome, never an inferred pass.

Rule ID: PROD-CIC. Standard approval requires no active CIC Group 3 or higher and no Group 2 in the preceding 12 months. A resolved Group 2 within 24 months may be one soft exception only if current obligations are clean, the CIC evidence is dated, and the exception follows [[APR-EXCEPTIONS]]. Active CIC Group 3 or higher is a hard stop.

Rule ID: PROD-RENEWAL. At renewal, the borrower must undergo a new product, underwriting, and approval review. Any material change or expiry follows [[APR-REAPPROVAL]]; the product does not automatically roll over.

## 5. Exception boundary

Rule ID: PROD-EXCEPTION-BOUNDARY. One soft exception produces Grade C and may address a history of 18–23 months, revenue from VND 8 billion to less than VND 10 billion, a resolved Group 2 within 24 months, or a qualifying underwriting metric under [[UW-SOFT-EXCEPTIONS]]. Exactly two soft exceptions are Grade C-EXCEPTION-2 and are eligible only for Tier 4 when every hard stop is absent, total exposure is > VND 3 billion and <= VND 5 billion, Risk and LegalCompliance concur, and the full rationale and mitigants are recorded under [[APR-EXCEPTIONS]]. Grade C-EXCEPTION-2 is not eligible for Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3. More than two soft exceptions are not permitted. Excluded industry or purpose, missing KYC or authority evidence, fraud or intentional misstatement, history below 18 months, revenue below VND 8 billion, active CIC Group 3 or higher, and all underwriting hard stops are non-overridable. No grade or tier may override a hard stop.
